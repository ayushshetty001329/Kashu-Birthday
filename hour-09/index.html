Some love is for the world. Ours is also for us.

I'm a very private person, babes. I know sometimes you want me to show more of us to the world, but honestly… I like us being private. Love in private. Kisi ki nazar na lage. ❤️ But being private never means loving you any less. Because when I'm with you, there is absolutely no privacy anyway. 😂 You get all of me. The stupid me. The emotional me. The clingy me. The annoying me. The version of me I don't show everyone. Maybe the world doesn't need to see every little thing about us. But you should always know this: My love for you is not private. It's endless. It's completely open to you. And it is yours. ❤️
Our love doesn't need an audience.
It just needs us.

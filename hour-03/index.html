The First Time
I Started Looking Forward to You

Some places don't just hold memories.
They quietly witness them.
One of the places that watched us become us.

I think one of the first things we really had in common was our love for ice cream. 🍦❤️

And somehow, Naturals became more than just a place where we went for ice cream.

It became one of those little places that quietly watched our story happen.

It saw us laugh. It saw us be stupid together. It saw us have our beautiful moments.

And maybe, it even saw us break a little sometimes.

But just like ice cream melts and somehow still leaves behind something sweet… we always found our way back to each other. ❤️

I think that's what I love about us.

We weren't perfect. We still aren't.

But somewhere between those ice creams, those conversations, those smiles and all those emotions… I started looking forward to seeing you.

And without even realizing it, I wasn't just looking forward to our next ice cream anymore.

I was looking forward to you. ❤️
My sweet lady, you'll always be my favourite flavour. 🍦

— Ayush ♡

The Little Things
I Fell For

Not one grand moment.
Just a thousand little reasons.
Tap the heart — I have something to tell you

The little thing I fell for most wasn't something you wore or something you said.

It was your happiness. ❤️

I love seeing you happy.

I love seeing you smile.

I love hearing you laugh.

There is something about seeing that smile on your face that makes everything around me feel lighter.

And somewhere along the way, I realised something very simple:

Your happiness
is my happiness. ❤️

So if I ever get one wish, it will always be to see that smile a little more.

Keep smiling, babes. You look beautiful when you're happy.

— Ayush ♡

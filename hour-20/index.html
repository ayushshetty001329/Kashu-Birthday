The Future I Imagine With You

We don't know what comes next. But I know who I want beside me.

Babe,

The future is something we're not completely sure about.

We don't know exactly where life will take us, what will change, or how everything will turn out.

But there is one thing I know.

I always imagine my future with you in it.

I think about the life we could build. The places we could go. The things we could achieve. The ordinary days that might become our favourite memories.

I don't know if everything we dream about will happen exactly the way we imagine it.

But I want to try.

I'll work for it. I'll fight for it. And if life gets difficult, I want us to face it together.

I want to stand with you when things are easy, and especially when they aren't.

I want us to keep choosing each other, keep growing, and keep fighting for the life we want.

I can't promise that the future will always be perfect.

But I can promise that, as long as we are given the chance, I'll stand beside you and give us my best.

Whatever comes next… I want to find out with you. ❤️

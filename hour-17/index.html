A Letter of Promises

A promise I want you to keep.

My Letter of Promises

Babe,

If I could promise you only a few things in this life, these would be them.

I promise to stay.

I promise to be there for you for as long as I can, through the good days and the difficult ones.

I promise to keep becoming better for you.

Not because you expect me to be perfect, but because loving you makes me want to become a better version of myself.

I promise to be better with you.

To listen more. To understand you better. To communicate better. To never stop learning how to love you.

I promise to make you laugh. 😂❤️

Even on the days when you don't feel like smiling, I'll do my best to bring that smile back.

I promise to look out for you.

To stand beside you. To support you. To remind you that you never have to face everything alone.

I promise to work hard.

For myself. For my dreams. And for the life I hope we can build together.

But there's one promise that's even more important.

I promise that I will always want you to become the strongest, happiest and best version of yourself.

I don't want you to become someone else. I want you to become more of you.

Chase your dreams. Believe in yourself. Grow. Become everything you've ever wanted to be.

And if you ever feel like you're not strong enough, I'll remind you of the girl I see.

A girl who is capable of so much more than she sometimes believes.

I don't know exactly what the future has waiting for us. But I know what I want.

I want to be there.

I want more mornings with you. More stupid fights. 😂 More random food dates. More movies. More travelling. More pictures. More hugs. More memories. More us.

And as long as life lets me, I want to keep choosing you.

Today. Tomorrow. And every day I get the chance. ❤️

Always yours,
Ayush

I don't promise you a perfect life.

I promise I'll keep trying to make it a beautiful one with you. ❤️

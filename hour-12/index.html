Why I Choose You

We're halfway through our 24 hours, and I think this is the perfect moment to tell you something simple.

I choose you because you are so much more than my lover. ❤️

You are my supporter.

My cheerleader.

My pillow when I need somewhere to rest.

My strength when I don't have enough of my own.

My coach when I need a push.

My English teacher when I say something completely wrong. 😂

And somehow, through all of that, you are still the person I want to come back to at the end of the day.

You make me want to become better — not because you ask me to be someone else, but because being loved by you makes me want to give you my best.

So if anyone ever asks me why I chose you...

Because with you, I don't just have a girlfriend.
I have my person. ❤️

— Ayush ❤️
